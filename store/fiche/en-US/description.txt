Jalonio puts your project management workspace in your pocket. The app supports teams that use Jalonio: find your tickets, move them forward and follow your projects' activity, wherever you are.

Every day
• "My tickets": what is assigned to you, sorted by due date
• Each project's tickets, grouped by status
• On iPad, the board in columns: drag a ticket from one status to another
• Change a ticket's status, assign it to yourself in one tap
• Read the description, sub-tasks and history
• Comment, and create a ticket in a few seconds
• Notifications: assignments, mentions, comments, due dates

Sovereign and respectful
• French publisher, data hosted in the European Union
• No advertising, no trackers, no third-party service in the app
• Secure sign-in, with two-factor authentication
• Works with the hosted workspace (your-organisation.jalonio.fr) as well as with an installation on your own servers

In six languages: French, English, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

Jalonio is simple project management: tickets, boards, sprints and milestones, understood without training. A Jalonio workspace is required: create one at jalonio.fr.
