Jalonio is a project management app for teams that already have a Jalonio workspace (accounts are created by invitation or on the website, never in the app).

Demo account, on the sign-in screen:
- Workspace: demonstration
- Email and password: see the fields above (no two-factor authentication)

Suggested tour (about 2 minutes):
1. Home: tickets assigned to you.
2. Projects > "Resident portal redesign": tickets grouped by status; on iPad, tap "Board" for the column view and drag a card to another column.
3. Open a ticket: change its status, assign it, add a comment.
4. Alerts: notifications. Account: sign-out, link to the web page for account deletion.

The demo workspace is reserved for review; changes are harmless. The app uses HTTPS only and contains no third-party SDK, advertising or tracking.
